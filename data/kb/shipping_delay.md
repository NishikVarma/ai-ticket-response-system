# Shipping and Delivery Delays
Customers can track an order from the Orders page by selecting Track Package. Tracking information is updated by the carrier and may lag by up to 24 hours.

If the package is more than 3 business days past the estimated delivery date, customers should contact the carrier with the tracking number and then contact support.

If a package is marked as delivered but not received, customers should check with neighbors and the building reception, and then contact support within 7 days.
